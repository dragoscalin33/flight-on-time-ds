# Brazilian Airport Guide for Flight Delay Analysis

## Major Airports and Delay Patterns

### São Paulo - Congonhas (CGH)
- One of Brazil's busiest airports, located in the urban center of São Paulo
- Known for frequent delays due to urban fog, especially early mornings (6-8am)
- Peak congestion hours: 7-9am and 17-19pm
- Short runway (1,940m) limits operations in bad weather
- Average delay rate: ~15% higher than national average
- Tip: flights before 10am and after 20pm tend to have fewer delays

### São Paulo - Guarulhos (GRU)
- Brazil's largest international airport
- Better infrastructure than Congonhas, handles larger aircraft
- Weather delays less frequent than CGH due to longer runways
- Main delays caused by air traffic congestion during peak hours
- Peak hours: 6-8am (domestic departures) and 21-23pm (international)

### Rio de Janeiro - Santos Dumont (SDU)
- Downtown airport, primarily domestic flights
- Located near Guanabara Bay - crosswinds can cause delays
- Fog common in winter months (June-August)
- Limited operations during heavy rain due to short runway
- Scenic approach over Rio makes it weather-sensitive

### Rio de Janeiro - Galeão (GIG)
- International airport, more resilient to weather than SDU
- Longer runways allow operations in moderate weather
- Further from downtown, taxi/transfer times longer
- Less congested than SDU for domestic routes

### Brasília (BSB)
- Modern airport with good infrastructure
- Dry season (May-September): minimal weather delays
- Wet season (October-April): afternoon thunderstorms cause regular delays
- Central location means many connecting flights

### Belo Horizonte - Confins (CNF)
- Located 40km from city center
- Fog frequent in early mornings, especially June-August
- Generally efficient operations once fog clears
- Mountain terrain can affect approaches in bad weather

### Salvador (SSA)
- Coastal airport, tropical weather patterns
- Heavy afternoon rain common December-March
- Generally good visibility and mild winds
- One of the better-performing major airports

### Recife (REC)
- Coastal airport with consistent trade winds
- Crosswinds can affect operations but rarely cause cancellations
- Tropical storms possible June-August
- Generally reliable for on-time performance

### Curitiba (CWB)
- Known for fog and cold weather, especially May-August
- Fog delays are the primary concern, particularly early morning
- When fog lifts, operations normalize quickly
- Southern location means occasional cold fronts

### Porto Alegre (POA)
- Southernmost major airport
- Cold fronts from Argentina can bring storms
- Winter fog similar to Curitiba
- Flooding has historically affected operations

## Seasonal Patterns

### Wet Season (October - March)
- Afternoon thunderstorms common across central and southeast Brazil
- São Paulo, Brasília, and Belo Horizonte most affected
- Morning flights generally more reliable during this period
- December-January: holiday traffic increases congestion

### Dry Season (April - September)
- Generally better for on-time performance
- Fog becomes the primary weather concern (especially June-August)
- Dry air can reduce visibility in some regions
- July: school holidays increase passenger volume

### Holiday Periods
- Carnival (February/March): major disruptions at all airports
- Christmas/New Year: highest traffic volume of the year
- July school break: moderate increase in delays
- National holidays: elevated traffic on surrounding days

## Airlines and Delay Patterns

### GOL Linhas Aéreas
- Largest domestic carrier by market share
- Boeing 737 fleet, point-to-point model
- Generally average on-time performance
- Strong São Paulo hub operations

### LATAM (formerly TAM)
- Largest overall carrier (domestic + international)
- Hub-and-spoke model increases connection complexity
- More affected by cascading delays from connections
- Strong international network

### Azul Linhas Aéreas
- Third largest carrier, strong in regional routes
- ATR turboprop fleet serves smaller airports
- Smaller aircraft more sensitive to weather
- Innovative with newer fleet aircraft
